Privacy Policy — gws-magicalne

Last updated: 2026-11-03

gws-magicalne is a personal, single-user command-line tool owned and operated by the account holder of magicalne@gmail.com. It connects to Google Workspace APIs (such as Gmail, Drive, Calendar) for personal automation on the owner's own machines.

What the application accesses

  • Metadata and content of the owner's own Google account (e.g. messages, files, events), as granted through Google's OAuth consent screens.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens, which are stored only on the owner's own devices in encrypted form.

What the application does NOT do

  • It does not collect, sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not store data on any server; all processing happens locally on the owner's machines.

Data retention

All credentials and data remain on the owner's local disk until deleted by the owner. Revoking the application's access in your Google Account immediately invalidates all tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy: magicalne@gmail.com.
